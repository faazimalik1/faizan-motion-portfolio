03 / About

Built around movement, clarity and rhythm.

I'm Faizan Malik, a Motion Designer and 2D Animator with 8+ years of experience creating visual content for digital platforms.

My work spans motion graphics, 2D animation, video editing, VFX and visual storytelling built for marketing, advertising, education and social media. I've worked across explainer videos, promotional content and brand-driven motion pieces, usually taking a project from concept and storyboard through to final edit.

Alongside motion work, I bring a design background in typography, infographics and branding, which shapes how I approach pacing, layout and visual clarity in everything I animate.
